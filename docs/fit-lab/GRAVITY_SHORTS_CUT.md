# Short support, gravity and closest garment — laboratory cut

Base: 1f846a49f1702729f813439c2746f484f4c22e29. Lab only; no commercial deployment or merge.

The previous short was authored on a canonical body, gathered over its upper 12 cm at runtime, and displaced in depth to clear the body. A render-only radial envelope then pushed the shirt outwards. This preserved an unwanted seat-like shape. Those production calls are replaced, not compounded.

The short now uses four sewn quadrants, one waist opening and two free hems. Waist, hip, length 34/42/50 cm and rise 33.5/35.5/37.5 cm are explicit fixed specifications, authored without any avatar input. This is pattern version 4.0, not a claim that its old geometry remains byte-identical.

A worker-side XPBD spring network uses area-weighted mass, gravity, stretch, bending, damping and vertex/face body contact. Only the waist boundary is pinned to a tied-drawstring support. Regional body changes modify the collider, not the short rest surface or seam lengths. The shirt solver contacts the current short triangles one-way. No radial render inflation or artificial rear length increase is used. Both garments share one revision and a joint cache/bootstrap; first physical frames and settling remain visible.

A nearest concrete garment proposal and its per-zone measurements remain available. Impossible contours or insufficient fixed rise are labelled for review, not silently stretched to fit. Fixed nominal cut length and observed vertical drape length are distinct.

Camera view buttons move into a translucent lower-left overlay. Existing bounded local cadera/pelvis/abdomen/muslo/pecho controls and free camera remain. Hairline is lowered/broadened at temples, with a lower tie and shorter falling ponytail, without editing body/collision geometry.

Reference images support optional front/side/back assets and follow the view command. The eight assets currently supplied are frontal: absent side/back views are labelled, not fabricated, mirrored or declared real. Creating matching directional reference images remains pending.

Material values and patterns are engineering hypotheses, not calibrated polyester or industrial approvals. No global self-collision or continuous collision detection is implemented. The adult MPFB base is not validated paediatric anthropometry. Tests cover topology, immutable rest geometry, waist attachment, gravity-versus-zero-gravity, local body edits, cache identity and browser/rendered joint presentation. Only observed tests and images may support release claims.
